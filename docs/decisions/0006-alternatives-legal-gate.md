# 0006 — "Dupes" / "Inspired by" feature behind a legal gate

Status: proposed — awaiting owner decision and legal advice

**Context.** Both "dupe" and "inspired by" wording that names a brand's perfume to sell or promote another
product carry trade mark and comparative-advertising risk in the UK/EU (e.g. L'Oréal v Bellure). Factual,
non-commercial similarity information is lower risk but still needs review.

**Proposal.** Build the feature as **Similar Perfumes**: factual note/accord similarity with scores, model version
and confidence; no "dupe"/"smells like"/"inspired by" marketing labels; no affiliate/price ranking mixed into
similarity. Launch only after legal sign-off.

**Consequences.** The feature (Phase 5) can be built and tested, but stays behind a feature flag in production until
approved.
